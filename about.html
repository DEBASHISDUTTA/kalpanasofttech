About us

Audit software that understands West Bengal.

KALPANA SOFTTECH SOLUTIONS builds practical software for one job done well: the statutory financial audit of West Bengal government schools — for the schools that prepare it and the auditors who verify it.

Why we built it

School audit preparation has long meant weeks of manual spreadsheets, recalculation and cross-checking — work that is exacting, repetitive and easy to get wrong. We started from a real school’s audit and rebuilt the whole process as software: read the source PDFs, apply the correct West Bengal rules, reconcile the numbers, and produce the exact forms an auditor expects.

The result turns a fortnight of preparation into a couple of hours, without asking anyone to trust a black box — every figure is reconciled and traceable back to its source.

How we work

✓

Offline first

Government financial records shouldn’t leave the office. Our software runs entirely on the user’s own machine.

✓

Accuracy over shortcuts

We follow the actual statutory forms and West Bengal GPF interest rules rather than approximating them.

✓

Built with real audits

Every feature is tested against genuine school data before it ships.
