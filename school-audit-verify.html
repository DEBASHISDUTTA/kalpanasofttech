Before you reach the school

A successful one-day audit begins with preparation

Every experienced auditor collects the school's documents in advance. This software turns that preparation into a structured, digital workflow — so your team walks in ready, not scrambling.

1

Select the school

Pick the school from your register and set the audit years. Each school is kept separate, organised and instantly available.

2

Load the documents

Feed in the salary statements, deduction records, bank statements and supporting registers — hard copy or soft copy — captured in the prescribed format.

3

Arrive prepared

Reach the school with everything structured and ready, so the audit itself is verification, not data entry.

Nothing left to loose paper

Everything a school audit demands — in the government format

Every requirement has a dedicated input, laid out exactly as the government format expects. Roll strength, results and the management period flow into Form 1 and Form 10; the asset list has its own comprehensive entry screen.
